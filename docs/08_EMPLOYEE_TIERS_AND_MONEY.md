# Employee Tiers & Money Flow

## Displayed vs Actual Commission

Tier 1 — Associate  
- Displayed: 20%  
- Actual: 10% of surplus fee.

Tier 2 — Specialist  
- Displayed: 40%  
- Actual: 20%.

Tier 3 — Senior Specialist  
- Displayed: 60%  
- Actual: 30%.

Tier 4 — Team Leader  
- Displayed: 80%  
- Actual: 40% + 5–10% override.

Tier 5 — Executive Partner  
- Displayed: 100%  
- Actual: 50% + 10–20% override.

Employees see their “rate” and payout.  
The system uses hidden base amounts and hidden multipliers (shadow accounting).
