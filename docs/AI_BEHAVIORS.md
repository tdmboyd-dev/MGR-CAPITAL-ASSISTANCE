# AI Behaviors — MGR Capital Assistance

## 1. Legal AI

- Reads state and county rules.
- Suggests required documents for each case.
- Generates motion, affidavit, POA, and cover letter templates.
- Validates deadlines and compliance.
- Never exposes surplus amounts or backend logic to employees or clients.

## 2. Employee AI

- Suggests call scripts based on case stage.
- Coaches employees on tone and clarity.
- Flags risky language or non-compliant statements.
- Suggests next best action for each case.

## 3. Client AI

- Answers common questions in plain language.
- Explains the process without exposing surplus industry details.
- Helps clients understand what to do next (upload ID, sign, wait, etc.).

## 4. Banking AI

- Suggests payout timing.
- Validates payout calculations.
- Flags anomalies in payouts or ledger entries.

## 5. Ingestion AI

- Reads tax sale lists and surplus PDFs.
- Extracts structured data.
- Creates internal cases.
- Flags high-value opportunities for Founder view.
