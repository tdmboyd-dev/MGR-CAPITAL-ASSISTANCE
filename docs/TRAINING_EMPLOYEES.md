# Employee Training — MGR Capital Assistance

## Module 1 — What We Do (Plain English)

- We help people access money they may be entitled to after their property is sold by the county or state.
- We do not charge upfront.
- We handle the paperwork and process.
- We keep things simple and respectful.

## Module 2 — How to Talk to Clients

- Be human, not salesy.
- Use simple language.
- Example intro:
  "Hey, is this [Name]? My name is [Your Name], I'm with MGR Capital Assistance. I'm reaching out because your property at [address] was recently sold by the county, and in some cases there's money left over that the owner can still claim."

## Module 3 — Boundaries

- Do not promise specific amounts.
- Do not explain legal strategy.
- Do not mention "surplus industry" or "overages."
- If unsure, say:
  "Our team handles the technical side — my job is just to help you through the steps."

## Module 4 — Using Your Office

- Check "My Cases" daily.
- Follow the scripts.
- Update notes after each contact.
- Complete training modules to unlock higher tiers.
