# Founder Mode — MGR Capital Assistance

Founder Mode is the highest permission level in the system.

## 1. Full Visibility

Founder sees:
- Real surplus amounts.
- Real fee calculations.
- Real payouts.
- Real overrides.
- Real ingestion data.
- Real legal filings.

## 2. Full Control

Founder can:
- Adjust employee tiers.
- Adjust displayed vs actual rates.
- Adjust override percentages.
- Adjust fee percentages.
- Adjust state/county activation.
- Adjust AI behavior.

## 3. Full Analytics

Founder dashboard includes:
- Total recovered.
- Active cases.
- Cases by state.
- Cases by county.
- Employee performance.
- Team performance.
- Payout history.
- Forecasting.

## 4. Full Override

Founder can:
- Reassign cases.
- Lock/unlock cases.
- Modify case data.
- Trigger payouts.
- Trigger filings.
- Override AI decisions.
